How it works

Runs on your Windows PC.

The desktop companion connects to Google after you grant permission in your browser. An iCUE widget displays your dashboard on the XENEON EDGE. A tray launcher keeps the local helper available without an open command window.

Your saved authorization can renew automatically. Google may still require reconnection if your authorization expires or is revoked.

Your information

A clear data boundary.

Google account credentials and local notes are protected on your PC with Windows encryption. Email and calendar information are held in local process memory while the app runs.

This public website explains the app. It has no Google sign-in form and does not receive your inbox, calendar, notes or account tokens.

About Google access

The standard connection requests your account email, Gmail read access and Calendar read access. It does not send mail, change email labels or edit calendar events.

About Google Keep

Live Keep sync is optional and uses an unofficial library with separate authentication. It is experimental and is not part of the standard Gmail/Calendar connection. Read the Keep-specific disclosure.
